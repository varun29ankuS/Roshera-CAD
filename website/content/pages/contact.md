Write to **[info@roshera.tech](mailto:info@roshera.tech)**.

For a **pilot**, include:

- a part you make today (STEP is ideal),
- the manufacturing process,
- what your drawings must show: standards, tolerancing, title block, projection method.

For **API or agent access**, tell us what you want your software to build or check.

We reply within two working days.
