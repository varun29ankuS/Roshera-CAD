Write to **[info@roshera.tech](mailto:info@roshera.tech)**. We reply within two working days.

## Engineering teams: a pilot

Include:

- a part you make today (STEP is ideal),
- the manufacturing process,
- what your drawings must show: standards, tolerancing, title block, projection method.

We return the drawing and its certificate.

## AI labs and agent teams

Tell us what your models need to build or be graded on: evals, an environment with verifiable rewards, or Roshera as the geometry tool behind your agent. See [For AI labs](/ai-labs).

## Everyone else
